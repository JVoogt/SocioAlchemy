## Brand Story

SocioAlchemy was born during South Africa's 2020 lockdown. When the world paused, T channelled her creativity into helping businesses find their digital voice. What started as a passionate side project under the name KaliYuki Social grew into a portfolio spanning hospitality, beauty, medical aesthetics, art, and adventure brands.

The rebrand to SocioAlchemy in 2025 marked a deliberate evolution — from freelance social media manager to digital storytelling alchemist. The name captures the core promise: transforming a brand's raw essence into digital gold.

### Timeline
- **2020** — First client signed during global uncertainty
- **2021** — KaliYuki Social officially launched
- **2023** — Portfolio expanded into hospitality, medical, beauty aesthetics, and art
- **2024** — Client representation at expos, brand management, on-site content shooting
- **2025** — Rebranded to SocioAlchemy

## Logo

### Primary Logo
Circular badge featuring a black ink-splatter astronaut figure, framed by a deep purple arc (top) and gold crescent (side). "SocioAlchemy" appears in a flowing script across the purple arc. The tagline "SOCIAL MEDIA. STRATEGY. SORCERY." runs along the bottom curve.

### Logo Symbolism
- **Astronaut** — Exploration, pushing boundaries, venturing into the unknown digital frontier
- **Ink splatter** — Creativity, artistic expression, making an impact
- **Circular frame** — Completeness, holistic approach to brand management
- **Purple + Gold** — Royalty meets alchemy; transformation of the ordinary into the extraordinary

### Logo Usage Rules
- Minimum clear space: half the logo height on all sides
- Never stretch, distort, or rotate the logo
- Use on white, off-white, black, or deep purple backgrounds only
- Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid backing
- The logo exists in two variants — use the tighter crop (logo2) for square social avatars

## Typography

### Headings
**Primary:** A flowing, elegant serif or script typeface (matching the logo script feel). Recommended: Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond.

### Body Text
**Secondary:** Clean, modern sans-serif for readability. Recommended: Inter, DM Sans, or Outfit.

### Accent Text
**Taglines/Quotes:** Italic variant of heading font for pull quotes and featured text.

### Type Scale
- Hero heading: 48-64px
- Section heading: 32-40px
- Subheading: 24px
- Body: 16-18px
- Small/Caption: 14px

## Visual Style

### Photography
- Warm, natural tones
- Behind-the-scenes and candid moments preferred over staged shots
- Golden hour lighting when possible
- Mix of lifestyle and product photography

### Graphic Design
- Clean, minimal layouts with generous white space
- Soft backgrounds (cream, blush) with bold text overlays
- Card-style content blocks with subtle borders
- Timeline/milestone elements for storytelling

### Imagery Themes
- Astronaut/space motifs (brand mascot)
- Alchemy references (transformation, gold, creation)
- Garden Route landscapes and local culture
- Authentic, lived-in brand moments

### Tone of Imagery
The brand visual identity walks a deliberate line between **bold and soft**. The astronaut logo is edgy and adventurous, while the content design is clean, feminine, and elegant. This duality reflects the brand promise: brave strategy delivered with refined creative execution.
