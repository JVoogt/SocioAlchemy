You **do not need a physical office address** to do this. Google supports "service-area businesses" — you hide your address and instead list the regions you serve.

## Step 1 — Create the profile

1. Go to **https://www.google.com/business/** and sign in with the Google account you want to own this listing. Use a Google account you control long-term — ideally one tied to `alchemist@socioalchemy.co.za` (set up Google Workspace, or a free Gmail you back up properly).
2. Click **Manage now**.
3. Search for *"SocioAlchemy"* — if nothing exists, click **Add your business to Google**.

**Important:** Don't use a personal Gmail you might lose access to. Whoever owns this account owns the listing.

---

## Step 2 — Business name & category

- **Name:** `SocioAlchemy` *(exactly — no keywords stuffed in. Google removes "SocioAlchemy — Social Media Marketing Knysna" type names.)*
- **Primary category:** `Marketing agency`
- **Additional categories** (add all that apply):
  - `Social media agency`
  - `Advertising agency`
  - `Graphic designer`
  - `Photographer` *(if you want to surface for content shoots)*
  - `Marketing consultant`

Categories are the single biggest ranking factor. Pick the primary that best matches what you want to be **found for**, not what feels broadest.

This is the critical fork. Saying "no" tells Google you're a service-area business. The "no address" option keeps your home address private but still lets you appear in local results.

## Step 6 — Verification

Google will offer one of:

- **Video verification** — most likely for a service-area business. You'll record a short walkthrough showing branded materials (laptop with site open, business cards, portfolio prints, branded notebook) plus your surroundings. Have these ready:
  - Laptop showing socioalchemy.co.za
  - Printed logo or business card
  - Any branded swag
  - Show the area you're filming in (helps prove you're in Knysna)
- **Postcard** — sent to a verification address (you give them, it stays hidden). Takes 5–14 days.
- **Phone/email** — sometimes offered.

**Tip:** Before recording the video, do a dry run. Google rejects unclear videos and you may have to re-shoot. Film in landscape, good light, narrate what you're showing.

## Step 8 — Get your first reviews (this is the unlock)

A profile with zero reviews ranks behind a profile with five — even if you're better. Plan a launch wave:

1. List 5–10 happy clients you've worked with. Beyond the Moon, Skinlogic, PRO-Installations, Etienne Steyn, Kampi team, Jane's, Adams.
2. Once verified, get your **review link** from the profile dashboard (Share profile → Get review link).
3. Send a short, personal WhatsApp:

   > Hey [name] — I just listed SocioAlchemy on Google and I'd love your honest review. Takes 30 seconds: [link]. Thank you for being part of this journey 🙏

4. **Don't blast all 10 in one day.** Google flags review velocity spikes. Spread over 2–3 weeks, 1–2 per day.
5. Reply to **every** review (good or bad) within 24 hours. Use the brand voice — warm, opinionated, never corporate.

**Do not pay for reviews. Do not write your own.** Google catches this and can suspend the listing permanently.

## Step 9 — Ongoing maintenance (15 min/week)

This is what 90% of businesses get wrong — they set it up, then ignore it.

| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| Weekly | Post a Google Update (treat it like a mini-Instagram post — new portfolio shot, client win, blog tip) |
| Weekly | Reply to any new reviews / questions |
| Monthly | Add 2–3 fresh photos |
| Monthly | Check the Insights tab — what searches are surfacing you? |
| Quarterly | Review services / hours / description for accuracy |

Google rewards activity. A weekly Update post is the single highest-leverage habit.

## Step 10 — Connect to your other assets

Once verified, link the profile from:

- **Website footer** — add a "Find us on Google" badge linking to your profile URL
- **Email signature** — `Reviews: [link]`
- **Instagram bio link** — via Linktree or your own link page
- **Facebook page** — add the Google profile to your "About" links

Cross-linking strengthens entity recognition (Google understands "this Instagram, this website, this Google profile = same business").

## Common pitfalls to avoid

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---------|--------------|
| Stuffing keywords in business name (`SocioAlchemy Social Media Knysna`) | Violates Google guidelines — listing gets reported and edited |
| Using a fake address to "look local" | Suspension. Service-area mode is the legit play. |
| Letting a third party "manage" with their email as primary owner | They can hold your listing hostage. Always own it yourself. |
| Asking for reviews in bulk on one day | Spam flag, reviews get filtered |
| Posting a review from your own Google account | Detected via IP/device, removed |
| Ignoring negative reviews | Compounds. A calm, professional reply often wins back the customer and impresses everyone reading it. |

## Success metrics — first 90 days

Track these in Google Business Profile **Insights**:

- **Searches** — direct (your name) vs. discovery (category searches like "marketing agency Knysna")
- **Profile views** → website clicks / calls / direction requests
- **Review count + average rating** — target: 10+ reviews at 4.8+ in 90 days
- **Photo views** — high views = your imagery is working

Realistic 90-day target: showing up in the **local 3-pack** for at least 2–3 long-tail searches like *"social media manager Knysna"* or *"content creator Garden Route"*.
